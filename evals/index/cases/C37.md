While I was showing the invoicing screens to a customer this morning I noticed two things, and I don't want them to get lost:

- when an invoice is sent to a customer whose email bounces, the invoice still shows as "Sent", with nothing telling the accountant that it never arrived;
- the list of overdue invoices doesn't let you filter by customer, and the accountants of the bigger clients scroll through three hundred rows every Monday.

Put them in our tracker so somebody picks them up. Invoicing has its own milestone this quarter, and the rest goes in the known-problems board. You have access to the tracker through its MCP.
