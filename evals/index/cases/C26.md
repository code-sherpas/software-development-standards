+# 17. Invoice and Customer change independently
+
+Date: 2026-11-02
+Status: accepted
+
+## Context
+
+Until v1, `Customer` held a list of its invoices and an invoice was saved by saving its customer.
+Two accountants editing two invoices of the same customer overwrote each other's work, and loading
+a customer with 4,000 invoices to change their postal address timed out.
+
+## Decision
+
+- `Invoice` and `Customer` are loaded, changed and saved on their own, each in its own transaction.
+- An invoice refers to its customer by `customerId` only. It copies the billing name, tax id and
+  address it needs at the moment it is issued; later changes to the customer do not alter an issued invoice.
+- `Customer` does not hold its invoices. “Invoices of a customer” is a query.
+- No rule today needs a customer and its invoices to change together. The credit limit check reads
+  the open total with a query and accepts that two invoices issued in the same second may both pass.
+
+## Consequences
+
+- The customer page lists invoices through `searchInvoices`, paginated.
+- If a rule appears that must hold across a customer and its invoices at the same instant, this decision is revisited.
