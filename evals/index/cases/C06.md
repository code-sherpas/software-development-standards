/build-prototype the onboarding prototype in `design/onboarding/` — the one the team reviews on Thursday.

Right now it's three static HTML pages linked with plain `<a>` tags: "Create your workspace", "Invite your team" and "Pick a plan". I need it clickable end to end, for two roles:

- **Admin**: creates the workspace, invites people by email (adds them to a list, can remove them before sending), picks a plan, lands on an empty dashboard with a checklist that shows what's done.
- **Member**: arrives from an invitation link, sets their name and password, and lands on the same dashboard but without the plan or billing parts.

There should be a role switcher in a corner so reviewers can jump between admin and member without restarting. The invite step needs the "email already invited" and "not a valid email" cases. Payment is not designed yet: the plan step can't really charge anything.

Check it works in desktop and mobile viewports before you hand it back; the reviewers will open it on their phones too. Use whatever you need to run and click through it.
