/refine — request from Joana, who runs pricing at Mercato:

> We need an audit of price changes. Several times this quarter a product's price changed and nobody could tell who did it or when. We'd like every change to a price — from the admin screen, from the bulk upload, or from the supplier sync — to be recorded with who made it, when, the old and the new value, and why if they gave a reason. Store managers should be able to see the history of a product's price, and only the pricing team should be able to see the history of all products at once. We have to keep it for five years for the tax office.
