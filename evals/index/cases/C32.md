/audit — the class booking area of Studiofy (booking a class, the waitlist, cancelling, and the instructors' attendance sheet). Next month we open it to the first three paying studios; until now only our own two demo studios have used it, and they were set up by us. I want to know whether it is really ready for studios we did not set up, with members and instructors we have never met.

Some context the team gave me:
- studio owners configure the classes and the cancellation window; instructors take attendance; members book and cancel from the web and from their phones;
- each studio can switch the waitlist on or off, and the two demo studios have it on;
- the area was built over a year by four different people, and there was never a design pass on it as a whole;
- we adopted a design system six months ago, but only the new screens use it;
- the demo studios live in production, and there are test accounts for an owner, an instructor and a member in each.
