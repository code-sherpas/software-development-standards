Increment 2 of PROJ-444 (“Payment reminders follow issued invoices”).

**Increment:** when an invoice is issued, the billing service publishes `InvoiceIssued` on the `billing-events` queue (SQS) and the reminders service schedules the first payment reminder. Both already exist; this increment cleans up the event: `amount` becomes `amountCents` (it always was cents, and a reminder once showed 125000 EUR), and `customer_id` becomes `customerId` like every other field. Both services are in this monorepo and deploy together from `main`, so the producer and the consumer change in the same pull request.

**Acceptance:**
- Issuing an invoice of 1,250.00 EUR schedules a reminder that says 1,250.00 EUR.
- A malformed event doesn't create a reminder.
