/to-increments PROJ-388 — it was refined yesterday, here is the task as it stands in the tracker:

> **PROJ-388 — Accountants export invoices as CSV, and customer phone becomes "contact phone"**
>
> **Why.** Accountants at our customers copy invoices by hand into their bookkeeping tools every month. Separately, sales keeps confusing the customer's `phone` with the phone of the person who signed the contract; the field is really the number of whoever we contact day to day.
>
> **What will be done.**
> 1. On the invoices page, a user with the *accountant* or *admin* role can export the invoices of a date range as a CSV file: number, issue date, customer name, net amount, VAT, total, currency, status. Up to 10,000 invoices per export; above that, the page asks to narrow the range.
> 2. The customer field `phone` is renamed to `contactPhone` everywhere: the `customers.phone` column, the domain model, the public API (`GET /customers/:id` returns `contactPhone`; `phone` keeps being returned, deprecated, for 90 days), the customer form and the CSV import template.
>
> **Terms.** *Contact phone*: the number of the person we contact day to day at the customer, not the signer's.
>
> **Decisions.**
> - The CSV uses comma separators, UTF-8 with BOM so Excel opens it, and amounts with a dot as decimal separator.
> - No change to who can see invoices.
> - The integration partners reading `phone` from the API were told on 2026-10-30.
>
> **Out of scope.** PDF export; exporting credit notes.
>
> **Acceptance.**
> - An accountant exports October's invoices and opens the file in Excel and in Google Sheets with every column right.
> - A customer created or edited after the change has its contact phone saved and shown; every existing customer keeps theirs.
> - API consumers still reading `phone` keep working for 90 days.

The app runs on Vercel with rolling deploys, Postgres via Prisma. Feature flags are in the flag tool we already use.
