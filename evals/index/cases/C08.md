/to-increments PROJ-605

> **PROJ-605 — "Summarize this thread" in support conversations**
>
> **Why.** Agents taking over a long conversation spend minutes reading it from the top before answering.
>
> **What will be done.** In the conversation view, a "Summarize this thread" button shows, above the messages, a summary of the conversation written by an LLM through the provider we already use for the help-centre search (`llm-gateway` client in `src/shared/secondary-adapter/`). The summary is shown as text and nothing else: it is not stored, it is not sent to the customer, it does not change the conversation's status or tags. It can be regenerated with the same button.
>
> **Terms.** *Thread*: every message of one conversation, customer's and agents', in order.
>
> **Decisions.**
> - The summary is written in the agent's interface language (English, Spanish or German), whatever language the customer wrote in.
> - Conversations of more than 200 messages are summarized from the last 200; the panel says so.
> - While it is being generated the panel shows progress; if the provider fails or takes more than 20 seconds, the panel says it could not be generated and offers to try again.
> - Behind a feature flag, on first for the internal support team.
>
> **Out of scope.** Suggested replies; summaries in the customer-facing widget.
>
> **Acceptance.** An agent opens a 150-message conversation, presses the button and reads an accurate summary in their language in under 20 seconds, with keyboard and with a screen reader as well as with the mouse.
