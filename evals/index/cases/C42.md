Task PROJ-508 «Reminders reach patients whose appointment moved», increment 1 of 1. Today the reminders worker sends each reminder from the copy of the appointment it stored when the clinic created it, so a patient whose appointment was moved gets the old time. Before sending, the worker must ask the clinic for the appointment as it is now.

Acceptance:

- A reminder states the appointment's current date and time, as the clinic has it when the reminder is sent.
- A reminder for an appointment the clinic has cancelled is not sent, and is marked as skipped.
- When the clinic cannot be reached, the reminder stays pending and the next run tries again.

The clinic's appointments are served by `clinic-web-service`, another application of our organization. From its README:

> `GET /appointments/{id}` → `200 { "id": "…", "starts_at": "2026-12-03T09:30:00.000Z", "status": "scheduled" | "cancelled" }`, `404` when the appointment does not exist.
