/fix-error BOOK-77 — the triage is done and it's in the task: https://tracker.example.com/BOOK/77. Go ahead with the fix.

Summary of what the task says, so you don't have to dig:

- **Error:** `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'zone')` on `POST /api/bookings`, 97 events since 28 October, all from the partner widget.
- **Root cause:** the partner widget sends `location: null` for clinics with a single site. The route handler passes the body straight to `toBookingRequest`, which reads `body.location.zone`. A booking without a location is valid: the clinic's own zone applies.
- **Also found:** 1,840 events in the same project, `BookingValidationError: slot is in the past`, are 400 responses to users who picked a slot that had just passed. They are the user's error and are reported as if they were the application's.
- **Owning layer:** the HTTP adapter that turns the widget's body into a booking request.
- **Proposed actions:** accept a missing location at the adapter and fall back to the clinic's zone; stop reporting validation 400s; regression test reproducing the widget's body.
